## 3. The 6 Architectural Pillars (What Clients Get)

### Pillar 1: AI Crawler Welcoming Protocol
- Replaces restrictive `robots.txt` with an explicit, high-priority welcome declaration for `GPTBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `PerplexityBot`, `Google-Extended`, and `CCBot`.
- Dispatches canonical `Sitemap: https://[domain]/sitemap.xml` headers directly to bot user-agents.

### Pillar 2: 5-Layer Schema.org JSON-LD Entity Graph
- Injects a deep semantic knowledge graph into every page header:
  1. `Organization` (Legal identity, logo, sameAs entity triangulation).
  2. `Person` (Principal founder authority, biography, credentials, external verification links).
  3. `ProfessionalService` / `LocalBusiness` (Catalog of high-ticket services offered).
  4. `SoftwareApplication` / `Product` (Proprietary software and service specifications).
  5. `FAQPage` (Pre-structured question-and-answer pairs designed for direct LLM quote extraction).

### Pillar 3: Persona-Matched Technical Content Engine
- Generates high-density, authoritative case studies matching the founder's authentic voice.
- Includes step-by-step systems architectures, Mermaid sequence diagrams, and real ROI benchmarks.
- Each article embeds dual `TechArticle` and `FAQPage` microdata so answer engines cite them as primary sources.

### Pillar 4: Google Indexing API Real-Time Bridge
- Automatically notifies Google's Indexing API endpoint via OAuth2 JWT service account authentication whenever new content is published.
- Reduces indexing latency from 3 weeks down to under 4 hours.

### Pillar 5: Multi-LLM Citation Radar & Telemetry
- Simulates high-intent buyer searches across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude.
- Calculates an objective **AI Citation Index (0–100)** tracking brand mention frequency and confidence.
- Automatically saves telemetry to client logs and detects competitive search gaps.

### Pillar 6: Authority Community Blueprint Seeder
- Generates authoritative discussion blueprints tailored for subreddits (e.g. `r/aws`, `r/HVAC`) and industry forums.
- Seeds trusted secondary third-party sources that LLMs crawl when forming consensus recommendations.
