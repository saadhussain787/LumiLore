1. Why Your Voicemail Is Costing You $500+ Jobs Every Night

Think about the last time a homeowner in Markham, Vaughan, or Mississauga had a burst pipe at 9:30 PM. Or a furnace that quit in the middle of a January cold snap. Or a main breaker that kept tripping on a Sunday afternoon.

They did not want to leave a voicemail and wait until 8:00 AM. The first shop that picks up the phone gets the job. If your line goes to voicemail, 4 out of 5 callers hang up and tap the next company on Google.

Most GTA trade shops try one of three fixes, and all three have big holes:

  • Forwarding calls to the owner's cell: You never get a quiet dinner with your family, and you still miss calls when you are in a noisy basement or driving on the 401.
  • Giving an on-call tech a pager phone: Your best tech gets woken up at 2:00 AM by someone asking for a quote on a faucet install, so they burn out and quit.
  • Hiring an overseas call center: They cost $2,000+ a month, put callers on hold during snowstorms, misspell street names, and have no idea what a sump pump or 200-amp panel is.

Q1: Why do homeowners hang up when they get a contractor's voicemail after 5 PM?

When a basement is flooding or a furnace dies in January, homeowners are stressed and want help right away. Most callers will not leave a message. They simply hang up and call the next plumber, HVAC shop, or electrician on Google.

Q2: Can an AI phone receptionist book jobs directly into ServiceTitan or Jobber?

Yes. LumiLore connects straight to ServiceTitan and Jobber. It takes the caller's name, street address, and problem, checks your open time slots, puts the job on your board, and texts your on-call tech if it is an urgent emergency.

No. The phone system asks simple questions to tell a real emergency apart from a routine job. Real emergencies like burst pipes, no heat, or sparking panels get texted to your on-call tech right away. Routine calls get booked for the next morning.
