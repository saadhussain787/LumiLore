Frequently Asked Questions

Real answers about telephony latency, human transfers, and CRM integration.

Does the voice agent sound robotic, or does it sound like a real person?

▼

Our telephony pipeline uses modern neural acoustic speech engines with sub-800 millisecond response times. It breathes naturally, understands conversational nuances and regional colloquialisms, and features full acoustic barge-in—meaning if a caller interrupts, the AI pauses immediately just like a human operator.

What happens if a caller asks a complex question the AI doesn't know?

▼

If acoustic confidence drops or a caller explicitly requests an on-call manager, the agent executes an immediate warm transfer to your on-call team line. Simultaneously, an SMS is sent to the manager with a 2-sentence summary of the caller's issue so they pick up with complete context.

Can we keep our existing business phone number?

▼

Yes. You do not need to change numbers. We provide a dedicated routing line, and you configure conditional call forwarding (e.g., forward when unanswered after 3 rings, or forward after 5:00 PM). Alternatively, we can port your primary number directly onto our AWS carrier mesh.

How does it integrate with ServiceTitan, Jobber, and Housecall Pro?

▼

We connect directly via secure OAuth2 REST webhooks. When an inbound customer calls, the AI inspects previous service history by matching the caller ID. When dispatching, it automatically opens a new job card, attaches audio call recordings and transcript summaries, and reserves the arrival slot on your dispatch board.

Are call recordings and customer data encrypted and PIPEDA-compliant?

▼

Yes. All telephony traffic and audio recordings are stored in AWS Canada Central (`ca-central-1`) with KMS 256-bit encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. We sign comprehensive Business Associate & Data Privacy Agreements with zero public LLM model-training covenants.
